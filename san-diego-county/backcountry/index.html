Backcountry & Desert

Private appraisals in 17 Backcountry & Desert communities.

The backcountry covers the eastern half of the county: Ramona, Julian, Pine Valley, Campo, Jacumba and Borrego Springs in the Anza-Borrego Desert. It ranges from Ramona's ranches and vineyards to Julian's mountain cabins and apple country, and down to desert homes surrounded by state park land. Distances are long and roads are winding, so we plan the visit around the drive and ask about access ahead of time. Sales are fewer, and each one has to be read carefully.

These markets work differently from the suburbs, and the drive alone takes planning. We make the trip. Backcountry assignments often involve estates, family transfers and properties that have been in a family for generations. The questions here are different from the city: well output, septic condition, road maintenance agreements, fire history and how far the nearest similar sale is. We ask those questions up front so the report covers them, and the fee reflects the extra research before you commit.

What makes appraising here different

Sales are thin, so finding good comparables often means searching over longer periods and wider areas, and the report explains why. Most homes rely on wells and septic systems, and water supply, well output and septic condition can change value considerably. Julian, Pine Valley and Ramona are in high wildfire risk areas, and fire insurance has become harder to get and more expensive. Many properties have outbuildings, manufactured homes or additions built without permits. In Borrego Springs, water limits and the seasonal desert market matter. Acreage, road access and topography have to be weighed carefully alongside the house.
