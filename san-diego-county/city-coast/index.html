San Diego Coast & Mesas

Private appraisals in 11 San Diego Coast & Mesas communities.

This region covers the city's beach neighborhoods plus the communities a short way inland: Carmel Valley, University City, Clairemont, Point Loma, Ocean Beach, Mission Beach, Pacific Beach and La Jolla. In a short drive you go from narrow beach lots and walk streets to post-war hillside tracts, 1960s and 1970s ranch homes and newer master-planned neighborhoods. Much of this area lies inside the coastal zone, and some of it sits close to Interstate 5 or the flight paths around the region, which buyers weigh.

We do a lot of estate and trust work here because long-time owners hold homes that have climbed far above their purchase price. We also appraise duplexes and small rentals near the beaches, where both rents and land value play a part in the number. Clairemont and University City homes come up often in trust work too, as original owners from the 1950s through the 1970s pass them on. Accurate square footage and permit history matter in all of these cases.

What makes appraising here different

Near the water, the land often carries most of the value, and small differences in view, alley access or lot width change the price. Mission Beach and parts of Pacific Beach have very small lots and many older homes that were added onto over time, so checking permitted square footage against what is actually there is part of the job. Point Loma and La Jolla have view homes where a partial ocean view and a full one sell far apart. Clairemont has many 1950s tract houses that have been expanded or rebuilt. Carmel Valley has newer homes, HOAs and Mello-Roos. Coastal zone rules also limit what owners can build.
