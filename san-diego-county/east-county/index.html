East County

Private appraisals in 19 East County communities.

East County covers El Cajon, La Mesa, Santee and Lakeside, plus unincorporated communities such as Spring Valley, Alpine and Jamul. Close in, you find post-war tracts and 1960s and 1970s neighborhoods. Farther east, lots grow larger, the hills get steeper and you see horse properties, ranchettes and custom homes on acreage. Many neighborhoods have views toward the mountains or across the valleys, and that can carry real weight in value.

Our work here includes estates, trusts, divorces and bankruptcy appraisals. Many owners are retirees who have lived in the same house for a long time, so the appraisal is often part of settling an estate or moving a home to the next generation. Others come from couples who need a fair value to split property. Rural and semi-rural homes come up often too, where outbuildings, wells and fire risk all need attention. We plan the research for each property type before the visit.

What makes appraising here different

The cities are mostly single-family homes on standard lots, with views adding real value in places like Mount Helix and the hills of La Mesa. Lakeside, Alpine and Jamul offer larger acreage with wells and septic, and many homes are in zones rated very high for fire hazard severity, which affects both insurance costs and how interested buyers are. Spring Valley has a wide mix of lot sizes and some homes with livestock zoning. Unpermitted additions and converted garages are common across the area. On rural parcels, usable land, road access and outbuildings often matter as much as the house itself, so comparable sales have to match on those features first.
