Client feedback

What we ask of clients, and why you will not find curated quotes here.

You will not find a page of hand-picked testimonials here. Curated quotes on a business's own site are easy to fake and hard to check, so we do not post them. If we have appraised a home for you, we would appreciate an honest Google review instead. Reviews there are public and verifiable, and they help the next person who is weighing whether to hire us. Below are the details that tend to help future clients most.

What helps the next client

  • What the appraisal was for, such as an estate, divorce, tax appeal or family transfer
  • Whether the quote, schedule and delivery date matched what you were told
  • How the inspection went and whether your questions were answered
  • Whether the report was clear enough for your attorney, CPA or the court to use
  • Anything you wish you had known before ordering
