Guides

Plain-language guides for estates, families and homeowners.

These guides cover questions that come up often in private appraisal work. They explain what a retrospective value needs, how an appraisal fits into a Proposition 19 family transfer, why an online estimate is not a substitute, and how to get ready for an inspection. They are general information, not legal or tax advice. Your attorney or CPA should guide the decisions.

Retrospective Value ChecklistWhat to gather when you need a home's value for an earlier date, such as a date of death or date of separation, and how the appraiser uses it.Proposition 19 Family TransfersHow California's Proposition 19 changed parent-to-child home transfers, why the home's market value on the transfer date now matters, and where a private appraisal fits in.Appraisal vs. Online EstimateWhy automated online home values often miss in San Diego County, what a certified appraisal does differently, and when each one is good enough for your purpose.Getting Ready for the InspectionA practical guide to preparing your home and paperwork for an appraisal inspection, what Brian looks at during the visit, and what does not matter.
