Appraisal types

Twelve reasons people order a private appraisal in San Diego County, and what each report has to do.

Date-of-Death AppraisalA retrospective value of an inherited home, pinned to the day the owner died, giving heirs and their CPA a stepped-up basis they can support.Estate and Trust AppraisalAppraisals for trust administration and estate settlement that help trustees value assets, split property fairly among beneficiaries and document their decisions for the accounting.Divorce AppraisalAn independent appraisal of the marital home or rental for a California divorce, valued as of the date your attorney or the court specifies.Bankruptcy AppraisalAn appraisal of your San Diego County home as of the petition date, used to support schedules, homestead exemption math and Chapter 13 plan payments.Property Tax Appeal AppraisalA lien-date appraisal that shows the market value of your San Diego County home as of January 1, for a decline-in-value review or formal assessment appeal.Family Transfer AppraisalAn appraisal for a home passing between family members by gift, sale or inheritance, supporting gift tax returns, Prop 19 exclusion math and fair pricing.Pre-Listing AppraisalAn independent appraisal before you list, so you can set a realistic price, weigh repairs and evaluate agent opinions with real numbers in hand.Pre-Purchase AppraisalAn appraisal for buyers who want an independent market value before or during escrow, especially for cash, family, off-market or unusual purchases.Insurance Claim AppraisalA market value appraisal for insurance disputes and property damage claims, documenting value before and after a loss or the lasting diminished value.Bail Bond Property AppraisalAn appraisal of a San Diego County home pledged as bail collateral, showing market value so equity can be measured against the bond amount.Immigration I-864 AppraisalAn appraisal of a sponsor's or joint sponsor's real estate, documenting market value so net equity can count toward the I-864 Affidavit of Support asset requirement.Expert Witness AppraisalAppraisals, appraisal reviews and expert testimony for real estate disputes heard in San Diego County courts and in mediation or arbitration, retrospective values included.
