What an appraisal costs

Published starting fees, and the things that can raise a quote.

Private appraisal fees start at $299 and go up with the amount of work the report requires. The price depends mainly on whether there is an inspection, what kind of property it is, and how hard the comparable research will be. You get a written quote before anything is scheduled, and that number stays fixed. Below is what moves the fee and how to choose the report that fits your purpose.

What changes the price

01

Inspection or no inspection

Desktop reports are done without visiting the home and cost less, starting at $299 for the Basic Desktop and $449 for the broader Desktop. A Drive-By, at $649, looks at the exterior. A Standard report with full interior and exterior inspection starts at $699. The visit adds travel, measuring and photos.

02

Property type

Single-family homes and condos are the starting point. Duplexes, triplexes and fourplexes need rent research and income analysis, so the 2-4 unit reports start at $549 for a Desktop and $799 with inspection. Homes with guest houses or ADUs may need extra work to sort out what is permitted and what counts.

03

Size, acreage and luxury

Large homes, view estates and properties on acreage usually have fewer sales to compare. That means a wider search, more adjustments and more explanation. Rural features such as wells, septic, groves, barns and long private roads also take time to research and describe. Those assignments are quoted above the starting fees.

04

Past effective dates

A retrospective appraisal, for a date of death or a date of separation, requires sales from around that date and a description of the home as it was then. That can mean digging through older listings, permits and photos. The further back the date, the more research it usually takes.

05

Court work and testimony

If the appraisal is headed for litigation, the report may need more detail and a closer review. Depositions, court testimony and meetings with attorneys are billed separately from the report. If you know a case may go to court, say so when you request the quote.

Rush work and testimony

If you need a report faster than the normal turnaround, rush service is often possible for an added fee. It depends on how full the schedule is, the property type and whether an inspection is needed. Desktop reports are the easiest to speed up. Let us know your deadline when you request a quote, and we will tell you plainly whether we can meet it and what it will cost.

Paying

The fee is due upon delivery of the report. You can pay by card, check or bank transfer. Law firms, CPA offices and other professional clients can arrange invoicing. The fee on your quote is what you pay, fixed before the work begins.

Choosing the right report

Start with what the report is for and who will read it. If you only need a reliable figure for family planning or an early talk with your attorney, a Basic Desktop at $299 or a Desktop at $449 is often enough. If the home's condition, remodel or additions affect value, or the report may be challenged, a Standard report with inspection at $699 is the safer choice. A Drive-By works when the inside cannot be seen. For duplexes and fourplexes, use a 2-4 unit report. When in doubt, ask. Brian will tell you which report fits, and he will not sell you more than you need.
