Clairemont

San Diego's big post-war suburb, now full of remodels and backyard ADUs

In 1950, developers Lou Burgener and Carlos Tavares recorded the first Clairemont tract map and began building what became the largest post-war subdivision in San Diego. The name came from Tavares's wife, Claire. More than seventy years later, those homes are still the backbone of the neighborhood, many of them remodeled, enlarged, or now sharing their lots with a second unit.

Clairemont covers the mesa between Interstate 5 and Interstate 805, from State Route 52 south to Linda Vista. It uses ZIPs 92110, 92111 and 92117 and includes North Clairemont, Clairemont Mesa East and West, Bay Park and Bay Ho. Canyons cut through the whole area, and how close a home sits to one, and whether it looks into it, affects value.

The housing stock

Most of Clairemont was built in the 1950s and 60s. The typical home is a modest single-level ranch house with stucco or wood siding and a low-pitched roof on a standard suburban lot. Builders laid out curving streets instead of a grid and placed many homes along canyon rims. Later decades added two-story homes, condo and townhome complexes, and apartment buildings near Clairemont Town Square and along Genesee Avenue, Balboa Avenue and Clairemont Mesa Boulevard. In recent years accessory dwelling units have become common on single-family lots, and the areas around the Balboa Avenue and Morena Boulevard trolley stations have seen new multifamily projects.

Value drivers to watch around Clairemont

01

Canyon rims

Homes on the rims of Tecolote Canyon or San Clemente Canyon often get privacy, open-space views and sometimes a long view west. They can also carry brush management duties and sit in mapped fire hazard areas, which affects insurance cost and availability. The appraisal weighs both sides using sales of other rim homes.

02

ADUs and second units

State and city rules have made ADUs common on Clairemont lots. A permitted, finished ADU can add value through rental income or flexibility, but buyers do not always pay its full construction cost. The appraisal values the ADU on what sales show and considers income where it fits the property.

03

Original versus updated

Clairemont has everything from untouched 1950s homes to full rebuilds. Original electrical, galvanized plumbing, single-pane windows and older roofs push value down, while modern systems and open layouts push it up. Accurate condition ratings from an interior inspection carry a lot of weight here.

04

Where on the mesa

Homes on the west side near the bay and I-5 often sell higher than those near I-805, and streets near the trolley or busy corridors differ from quiet cul-de-sacs. The appraisal draws comparables from the same part of Clairemont whenever possible instead of from the whole community.

Appraisals people order here

With original owners and their children still in many houses, Clairemont is one of the top areas for estate, trust and date-of-death work, usually for heirs weighing whether to sell, lease or hold on to the home. Family-transfer appraisals matter here too, especially under Proposition 19 rules for parent-to-child transfers. Divorce and bankruptcy appraisals come up for homes where equity is the main asset. The Standard $699 with interior inspection is the usual fit. Properties with an ADU or a second home on the lot may call for the Standard 2-4 Unit at $799, depending on how they are set up.

Asked about Clairemont

Why would I need an appraisal for a parent-to-child transfer?

With Proposition 19, how a parent-to-child transfer is taxed hinges on what the home is worth on the transfer date and whether the child makes it a primary residence. A dated appraisal documents that market value for your records, your CPA and your attorney.

Will an ADU on my Clairemont lot be valued as a rental?

It may be. If the ADU is permitted and rentable, the appraisal considers market rent and sales of similar homes with ADUs. If it is a garage conversion without permits, it is described but credited carefully, since buyers usually discount unpermitted space and the cost of making it legal.

Do you use sales from all of Clairemont?

Only when needed. Clairemont is large, and values shift between the west side near the bay, the canyon rims and the eastern mesa near I-805. The appraisal starts with the closest similar sales and widens the search only when there are not enough of them.
