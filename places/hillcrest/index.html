Hillcrest

Uptown condos, 1920s bungalows and canyon lots just north of Balboa Park

Start at the intersection of University and Fifth Avenue, under the Hillcrest sign, and you are in the middle of one of the older streetcar suburbs in the city. Hillcrest grew up in the 1910s and 1920s around streetcar lines heading north from downtown. It is part of the Uptown community planning area along with Mission Hills, Bankers Hill and University Heights.

Hillcrest has more condos and apartments than most nearby neighborhoods, and it is home to UC San Diego Medical Center's Hillcrest campus. Its housing is mixed: a 1920s Spanish Revival duplex might sit next to a 1970s apartment building and a newer mixed-use project. For you, that means comps need care. A condo in a 1960s building and a newer unit two blocks away are not the same product, even with the same square footage.

The housing stock

Most single-family homes date to the early 1900s, including Craftsman bungalows and Spanish Revival houses plus a few Prairie and Mission style designs, and many sit on canyon-backed lots close to Vermont Street and the rim of Mission Valley. Small apartment courts and duplexes from the 1920s and 1930s are common. Postwar decades added two- and three-story stucco apartment buildings, many later converted to condos. Since the 2000s, mixed-use buildings with condos above retail have gone up along University Avenue, Park Boulevard and Fifth Avenue. Many condos have small HOAs with limited reserves, so documents matter.

Value drivers to watch around Hillcrest

01

Condo conversions and small HOAs

Many Hillcrest condos were converted from apartments. Older conversions can have shared plumbing, small reserve funds and limited parking. Buyers notice, and so do their lenders. We review the HOA's dues, reserves and any pending assessments when they are available and compare against conversions of similar age.

02

Canyon edges

Homes backing onto canyons get privacy and sometimes views toward Mission Valley, but slope, retaining walls and fire hazard zones can offset those gains. We look at how usable the yard actually is and how similar canyon lots in the area have sold.

03

Parking

Street parking is tight. A house with a garage or off-street spaces, or a condo with deeded parking, often trades higher than one without. We confirm parking for the subject and each comparable.

04

Historic resources

Some older houses fall within or near designated historic districts or are individually designated. Designation can limit exterior changes but can also allow Mills Act property tax savings. We note any designation and how it affects the likely buyer.

Appraisals people order here

Hillcrest owners often ask for divorce and estate-trust appraisals, especially for condos and duplexes held for many years. Date-of-death values are common when an older home passes to heirs. Investors with small multi-unit buildings use the Standard 2-4 Unit or Desktop 2-4 Unit reports for buyouts and family transfers. Pre-purchase appraisals help buyers check a price on an older conversion before committing. For condos in buildings with steady sales, a Desktop report can be enough. Older houses and duplexes usually call for an interior inspection.

Asked about Hillcrest

Can you appraise a duplex or fourplex in Hillcrest?

Yes. We offer a Desktop 2-4 Unit report starting at $549 and a Standard 2-4 Unit report with an inspection starting at $799. We look at both comparable sales and rents, since buyers for small multi-unit buildings pay attention to income.

Does being near the medical center affect value?

Being close to the hospital is a draw for staff who want to walk or bike to work. Busier streets, ambulance routes and parking demand can work the other way for some homes. We look at sales close to the campus to see how buyers have treated it.

My condo is in an older converted building. Is that a problem?

Not in itself, but it changes which sales we use. We compare first to other conversions of similar age and size, then adjust for differences in parking, laundry, dues and updates. If you have HOA documents, send them along.
