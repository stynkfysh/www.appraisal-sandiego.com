Paradise Hills

Postwar ranch homes on hills with views toward San Diego Bay

Climb the hills of Paradise Hills on a clear day and you can see across San Diego Bay to Coronado and downtown. That view, plus quiet residential streets, is a big part of the area's appeal. Many homes here were built in the 1950s and 1960s, and many families have stayed for decades. Streets curve around the hills, and many houses have yards facing west toward the water.

The neighborhood sits in the southeastern corner of the City of San Diego, near National City and Bay Terraces, with a large Filipino-American community. For an appraisal, the key differences between homes often come down to view, lot position on the hills and how much the home has been updated since it was built. Location on the slope, and whether a home looks west or at a neighbor's roof, can matter more than you might think.

The housing stock

Most homes are single-story ranch houses built from the 1950s through the 1960s, usually three or four bedrooms and around 1,100 to 1,700 square feet. Typical lots are roughly 5,000 to 8,000 square feet, while hillside lots tend to be bigger and sloping. Many homes have been expanded or remodeled, while others remain close to original. There are also some newer homes and townhomes, especially toward Bay Terraces, where some developments have HOAs. Most older detached homes have no HOA. Pools and patio covers are common additions.

Value drivers to watch around Paradise Hills

01

Bay and city views

Homes on higher streets can look across San Diego Bay toward Coronado and downtown. These views often carry a meaningful premium. The appraiser compares view homes with other view sales to measure it. Views blocked by trees or neighboring homes may carry less premium.

02

Hillside lots

Sloped lots may offer views and privacy but less usable yard. Slopes can also complicate additions or ADUs. The appraisal considers both the benefits and limits of each lot. Retaining walls and drainage are also worth checking on steep sites.

03

Expansion and remodeling

Many homes have been enlarged or updated. Permitted additions add value, while unpermitted space may get less credit. Condition and updates often explain the biggest price differences between similar homes. An interior inspection usually tells the story. The appraiser checks this on site and in the sales data.

04

Freeway and corridor access

Commuters benefit from fast connections to State Route 54 and Interstate 805. Homes near busy roads may sell for less than those on quiet streets, so location within the neighborhood matters. Proximity to shopping and schools can also help. The appraiser checks this on site and in the sales data.

Appraisals people order here

Heirs taking over from original owners are one of the biggest sources of requests here. Family transfers are very frequent, often within extended families. Immigration I-864 appraisals sometimes come up when a homeowner sponsors a relative and needs to document home equity. Divorce and pre-listing reports also appear. A Standard report usually fits best, especially for view homes, though a Desktop can work for some planning purposes when condition is well documented.

Asked about Paradise Hills

Can a home appraisal help with an I-864 Affidavit of Support?

Yes. An appraisal can document the market value of your home so you can show equity as an asset for the sponsorship. A Desktop or Standard report is often used. The report states value as of a specific date, which you can then use with your mortgage balance.

How much do bay views add to value in Paradise Hills?

It depends on the view's width, quality and permanence. The appraiser compares with recent sales of homes that have similar views and those that don't, using actual buyer behavior rather than a fixed percentage. The premium can be meaningful for wide, open views.

Do you compare Paradise Hills homes with National City sales?

Sometimes, when nearby National City homes are similar. The closest, most similar sales get priority, and the report spells out the reason each comparable was used. Neighborhood boundaries and buyer preferences are also considered when choosing them. The report spells out the reasoning so you can follow it.
