National City

Victorian roots, post-war bungalows, and three freeways in nine square miles

National City became a city in 1887, making it among the oldest in San Diego County, and you can still see that history. You can drive from 1880s Victorians near Heritage Square to 1950s ranch homes in the El Toyon area in a few minutes. I-5, I-805 and State Route 54 all run through the city, and Naval Base San Diego lines the bay along its west side.

Small lots, older homes and a high share of rentals make National City a value-conscious market where details count. A garage conversion, a second unit in back, or a house two blocks closer to the freeway can move value more than you would expect. The appraisal needs nearby sales that match on age, lot, unit count and street, because a mile in National City can cover very different blocks.

The housing stock

The oldest housing is near downtown and along A Avenue, where the Brick Row townhouses at Heritage Square date to the 1880s, along with scattered Victorian and Craftsman homes. Much of the city filled in during the 1920s through the 1950s with small bungalows and stucco cottages, many of which now have additions. El Toyon and the eastern hills near Plaza Bonita have more 1950s and 1960s ranch homes, some with bay views. You will find duplexes, triplexes and small apartment complexes across the whole area. Condos and newer infill townhomes have gone up near the trolley stations and along the Highland Avenue and National City Boulevard corridors.

Value drivers to watch around National City

01

Second units and conversions

Many lots have a second unit in back or a garage turned into living space. Permitted units add income and value. Unpermitted ones are described honestly and usually valued at less, because a buyer takes on the risk. Bring any permits, plans or finals you have.

02

Freeway and industrial exposure

Homes right next to I-5, I-805 or SR-54, or near industrial zones close to the bay, can trade at a discount for noise and air quality. A few blocks away the effect fades. The report compares sales with similar exposure or adjusts for the difference.

03

Historic homes

Victorian and early Craftsman homes draw a different buyer than a 1950s stucco house. Original details, condition and any historic designation are noted. Good comparables may be scarce, so the search can widen to older homes in neighboring communities.

04

Unit count and rent

Duplexes and small multifamily lots are common. For two to four units, value depends partly on rents and on what similar income properties sold for. Leases, rent rolls and any recent upgrades help the report reflect what an investor would pay.

Appraisals people order here

National City sees a steady mix of estate-trust and date-of-death appraisals for long-held family homes, plus family transfers when relatives buy out siblings. Immigration I-864 appraisals are common, since a sponsor's home equity can help meet the income test. Bankruptcy filings and bail-bond collateral also come up. Many properties have more than one unit, so the Desktop 2-4 Unit report at $549 or the Standard 2-4 Unit at $799 is often the right call. A single-family I-864 or estate file can often be handled with a Desktop report at $449.

Asked about National City

My lot has a house in front and a unit in back. Is that a 2-4 unit appraisal?

If both are legal dwelling units, yes. The 2-4 unit report looks at income as well as sales. If the back unit is unpermitted, the property is usually appraised as a single-family home with the extra space noted separately. Permit records settle the question.

Can my National City home equity count for an I-864 sponsorship?

Home equity can count as an asset in some cases. USCIS generally wants a current appraisal to establish market value. A Desktop appraisal is often accepted for this purpose. Check with your immigration attorney on what they want to see.

Will freeway noise lower my appraisal?

It can, if your home is close enough to feel it. The report compares your home with sales that have similar exposure. If nothing similar sold, it uses paired sales to estimate how much buyers discount for that location.
