Mission Hills

Historic hillside homes above Old Town with bay, airport and valley views

Kate Sessions, the horticulturist behind much of Balboa Park's planting, ran a nursery in Mission Hills in the early 1900s, and the trees she favored still shade many of its streets. The neighborhood sits on the bluffs above Old Town and Mission Valley, and its western edge looks out over the bay, the airport and Point Loma. Development began around 1908, and many homes went up in the 1910s and 1920s.

This is one of the city's most architecturally rich residential areas. Homes in this area were designed by architects such as Richard Requa, Irving Gill and William Templeton Johnson. Some blocks fall within historic districts. When you appraise a Mission Hills house, the view, the lot, the architect and the level of restoration can each move value as much as the square footage does.

The housing stock

Expect Craftsman bungalows, Spanish Colonial Revival, Mission Revival, Prairie, Tudor and some Victorian-era homes, mostly built between about 1908 and 1940. Lots vary from narrow urban lots to larger hillside parcels with terraced yards and canyon frontage. Postwar infill added some mid-century modern homes and small apartment buildings, mainly near Washington Street and the commercial strip. There are a few condo projects but far fewer than in Hillcrest. Designated historic districts include the Fort Stockton Line and the Mission Hills district around Sunset Boulevard, and many homes have Mills Act contracts.

Value drivers to watch around Mission Hills

01

View premium

A home with an unobstructed view of the bay, downtown or Mission Valley can sell well above a similar home without one. We document the view from the main living areas and compare against sales with similar views. A partial view through trees is valued differently from a wide panorama.

02

Historic designation and Mills Act

Designated homes can qualify for Mills Act contracts, which can lower property taxes. Those savings transfer to a buyer and can support a higher price. Designation also limits exterior changes. We note any contract and how comparable designated homes have sold.

03

Architect and restoration quality

A well-restored home by a known architect draws a specific group of buyers. An older home with poorly matched additions may not. We look at materials, craftsmanship and how faithfully original details have been kept, and we compare to homes of similar quality.

04

Canyon slopes and fire risk

Canyon lots give privacy but can mean steep yards, retaining walls and placement in a fire hazard severity zone. Those can affect insurance and buyer interest. We weigh usable yard area and slope against nearby canyon sales.

Appraisals people order here

Here, estate, trust and date-of-death reports rank among our top requests, because so many families have owned their homes for decades and are sitting on big gains. Divorce appraisals come up for high-value homes where precise valuation matters. Family-transfer appraisals help with Proposition 19 planning. Insurance-claim valuations sometimes come up after fire or water damage to older homes. For historic or view homes, a Standard report with a full inspection is usually the best choice, because the details that drive value need to be seen and recorded.

Asked about Mission Hills

Can you value a historic Mission Hills home accurately?

Yes. We look for sales of other designated or architecturally significant homes in Mission Hills and nearby Uptown neighborhoods. We note Mills Act contracts, original features and the quality of any restoration, and we explain how each was weighed.

How do you put a number on a view?

We compare sales of similar homes with and without comparable views and use the price difference as a guide. The type of view matters: a wide bay view usually carries more weight than a partial valley view through trees.

Does airport noise affect Mission Hills values?

Parts of western Mission Hills are under or near the approach path to San Diego International Airport. The effect varies by street. We compare against sales with similar exposure to measure it.
