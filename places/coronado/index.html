Coronado

Craftsman cottages, Ocean Boulevard estates, and Navy jets overhead

You get to Coronado over the San Diego-Coronado Bridge or along the Silver Strand, and it has been a separate city since 1890. The village took shape around the Hotel del Coronado, which opened its doors in 1888, with streets laid out in a grid and lined with trees. Naval Air Station North Island takes up the north end, and Coronado Shores and Coronado Cays stretch south along the Strand.

Values in Coronado swing more by exact location than almost anywhere else in the county. A house on Ocean Boulevard, one on a quiet village street and a condo in a Shores tower are three different markets. Lots are small and expensive, so a lot of the value is in the land. An appraisal has to pin down the block, the view, the lot and the house itself, and support each piece with local sales.

The housing stock

The village has a deep stock of early 1900s cottages and Craftsman bungalows, Spanish Revival homes from the 1920s, and later ranch and contemporary rebuilds. Many original homes have been replaced or expanded, so a single block can mix a 1915 cottage with a new two-story. Ocean Boulevard and the Country Club area near Glorietta Bay hold large homes and estates. Coronado Shores, a group of high-rise condo towers built in the 1970s, sits on the beach south of the Hotel del Coronado. Coronado Cays, also begun in the 1970s, is a gated community of homes and townhomes on canals, many with private docks. Condos and duplexes are mixed into the village too.

Value drivers to watch around Coronado

01

Land value and lot size

Village lots are small, and land carries a large share of each sale price. An older cottage on a full lot may sell close to land value. The appraisal looks at whether a buyer would keep, expand or replace the home, and supports that with recent sales of similar lots.

02

Ocean, bay and skyline views

Views of the Pacific, the bay, the downtown skyline or the bridge drive large premiums. The size and permanence of the view matter. Front-row Ocean Boulevard homes, Glorietta Bay frontage and upper-floor Shores units are compared with sales that have similar exposure.

03

Historic resources and remodel limits

Coronado maintains a historic resource program, and some homes are designated or eligible. Designation can bring Mills Act property tax savings but also limits on changes. Buyers weigh both. The report notes any known designation and how it compares with the sales used.

04

North Island flight activity

Military aircraft operating from Naval Air Station North Island bring noticeable noise to parts of the island. Some buyers accept it easily, others discount for it. Location relative to the base and flight patterns is considered when choosing comparables.

Appraisals people order here

Coronado brings a high share of estate-trust and date-of-death appraisals, because many homes have been held by families for decades and step-up in basis is worth real money at these values. Divorce and expert-witness appraisals are also common, since the stakes are high. Family transfers come up when a beach home passes to the next generation. Many military families buy and sell here, which leads to pre-listing and pre-purchase work. Luxury and complex properties can be quoted above the starting fee. A Standard appraisal with full inspection is the usual choice, and retrospective date-of-death work is handled regularly.

Asked about Coronado

Is a teardown cottage in Coronado appraised as land?

Not automatically. The report looks at what a typical buyer would do with the property. If recent sales show buyers paying land value and replacing similar homes, that is reflected. If the cottage adds value as is, it gets credit. The reasoning is explained in the report.

Can you appraise my parents' Coronado home for an earlier date for the estate?

Yes. Looking back in time is ordinary work, and these reports as of a date of death are routine. I rely on sales close to that date and describe the house as it stood then, drawing on photos, public records and what the family can tell me. Retrospective work can cost more and is quoted up front.

Do you appraise condos in Coronado Shores and homes in Coronado Cays?

Yes. Shores condos are compared with units in the same or similar towers, adjusted for floor, view and size. Cays homes are compared with other Cays sales, with attention to water frontage, dock size and channel location.
