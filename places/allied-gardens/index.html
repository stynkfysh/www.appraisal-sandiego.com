Allied Gardens

Postwar ranch homes, Waring Road and quick access to Mission Trails

Built out mainly in the mid-1950s, Allied Gardens got its name from the group of builders who developed it. The result is a neighborhood of single-story homes on gently sloping streets, with a community park and pool at the center and Grantville's shops and trolley stop at the bottom of the hill. Those features still drive demand, and families who grew up here often come back to buy.

Located east of I-15 and north of I-8, it belongs to the Navajo planning area together with Grantville, San Carlos and Del Cerro. Mission Trails Regional Park is a short drive. The housing is consistent enough that you can compare homes cleanly, but there are real differences between streets with views and streets near Waring Road traffic, and those differences show up in what buyers pay.

The housing stock

The typical home is a one-story ranch from roughly 1954 to 1960 with three bedrooms, one or two baths and 1,000 to 1,500 sq. ft. of living area. Parcels usually fall near 5,000 to 7,000 sq. ft., growing larger and steeper on the hillside streets. A fair number have been expanded or remodeled, and some have added second stories or ADUs. Homes on the upper streets can have views across the valley. Attached housing is limited, with a few condo and apartment buildings near Mission Gorge Road and Grantville. Most detached homes have no HOA.

Value drivers to watch around Allied Gardens

01

Hillside views

Upper streets toward the north and east can look out over the valley and nearby hills. View homes draw more interest and tend to sell higher than similar homes on flat interior streets. The appraiser compares view sales to view sales whenever the data allows.

02

Busy street exposure

Homes fronting Waring Road, Zion Avenue or near Mission Gorge Road deal with more traffic and noise. Such homes tend to sell for less than comparable ones on quiet interior streets, so where a home sits in the neighborhood counts as much as its size.

03

Expansion potential

Lots here can often support a room addition, second story or detached ADU. Homes that already have added permitted space usually command more, and buyers sometimes pay for the potential on a larger lot. Permits matter, since unpermitted space gets less credit.

04

Brush and slope

Some streets back onto canyons and open space toward Mission Trails and the river. These can fall within fire hazard severity zones, which affects brush clearance and insurance availability. I note the designation in the report and search for comparable sales that share that same exposure.

Appraisals people order here

Many homes here were bought decades ago and are now passing through trusts, so date-of-death and estate-trust appraisals come up often. Divorce appraisals are common for families who bought here for the schools and the park. Family transfers between parents and children are also frequent, sometimes tied to property tax planning. Pre-listing reports help sellers price homes with additions or partial updates. A Standard report usually fits best, since condition and remodeling drive much of the value difference between similar homes.

Asked about Allied Gardens

Are Allied Gardens homes appraised against Del Cerro or San Carlos sales?

Usually the closest and most similar sales come from Allied Gardens itself. Del Cerro and San Carlos sales may be used when the home has features, such as a larger lot or a view, that are more common there. In the report, you'll see the reason each comparable was selected.

Will a second-story addition raise my appraised value?

Permitted added living area generally adds value, though not always dollar for dollar against what it cost to build. The appraiser looks at sales of similar expanded homes in the neighborhood to see how buyers have actually priced that extra space.

How quickly can I get an appraisal for a trust?

Timing depends on the schedule and report type, and rush service is available for an added fee. A Desktop report can often be completed faster than a Standard report because no inspection is needed. The fee and timing are confirmed before anything is scheduled.
