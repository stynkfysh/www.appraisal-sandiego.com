Boulevard

High desert ranches and boulder fields between Interstate 8 and the border

Out past Campo, where Old Highway 80 runs alongside Interstate 8, the land opens into wide high-desert basins dotted with boulders, scrub oak and manzanita. Boulevard covers a big area with a small population, and it includes several named places: Manzanita, Live Oak Springs, Tierra del Sol, Jewel Valley and others. Wind turbines on the ridges are now part of the view.

If you are valuing property here, start with this fact: Boulevard sales are few, and the properties are very different from one another. A cabin at Live Oak Springs, a newer home on twenty acres in Tierra del Sol and an older trailer on a small lot near Old Highway 80 do not compete with each other. A good report treats them separately and tells you why.

The housing stock

Housing in Boulevard is spread across large parcels, many from four to forty acres or more. You will see older cabins and small homes from the mid-1900s, ranch-style houses from the 1970s through the 2000s, owner-built homes, and many manufactured homes. Some newer custom homes take advantage of open views. Outbuildings, water storage tanks, solar panels and off-grid setups are common. Condo complexes and HOA communities are essentially absent, and homes depend on private wells and septic. Some properties are only partly finished or have been expanded over many years.

Value drivers to watch around Boulevard

01

Off-grid and solar setups

Some homes run on solar with batteries, generators and hauled or stored water. Others are fully connected to power. A well-designed off-grid system can be valuable, but it also narrows the pool of buyers. The appraisal notes the system and whether it is owned or leased, then compares it with similar properties.

02

Wind and energy projects

Wind turbines and transmission lines are part of the Boulevard landscape. Some buyers do not mind them; others do. A view of turbines or being close to power lines and equipment can reduce appeal, so I note these items in the report and, when I can, find comparable sales with the same exposure.

03

Groundwater dependence

Boulevard relies entirely on groundwater. Well depth, yield and condition directly affect value. In dry years, a weak well can become a serious drawback. Any records of drilling, yield tests or recent pump work help support the value and reduce assumptions.

04

Remote location

Distance from shopping, schools and medical care limits the buyer pool. Being close to Interstate 8 or Old Highway 80 on a paved road helps. A property deep on a dirt road may take longer to sell and usually sells for less per acre than a comparable parcel with easier access.

Appraisals people order here

Boulevard requests often come from families closing out an estate on land they held for many years. Divorce cases may involve dividing acreage or deciding what to do with a rural home. Bankruptcy assignments come up where a fair value for remote land matters to a trustee. Family-transfer appraisals help when parents pass land to children. Because each property is different and sales are thin, a Standard appraisal with a site visit is usually best. Large or complex acreage may cost more.

Asked about Boulevard

Can you appraise an off-grid home in Boulevard?

Yes. I document the power, water and septic setup, including solar panels, batteries, generators and storage tanks. I look for other off-grid sales to compare and explain adjustments where the comparables differ. Records of system age and ownership help keep assumptions to a minimum.

How do you handle very large parcels?

Large acreage is valued by looking at sales of similar size and use, often across a wider area. I separate the value of the home and improvements from the land where it helps explain the result. Fees for larger or complex properties are quoted before scheduling.

Is vacant land in Boulevard something you appraise?

This site focuses on residential property, but land with a home or with clear residential potential can be appraised. Access, water availability and zoning all matter. If the land is vacant, I will tell you up front whether it fits the type of report you need.
