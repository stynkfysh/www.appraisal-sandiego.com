North Park

Craftsman bungalows, 30th Street shops and a deep bench of comparable sales

Walk a few blocks off University Avenue and you are on streets of Craftsman bungalows, many built between 1910 and 1930 when streetcars connected the area to downtown. North Park sits just northeast of Balboa Park, and the 30th Street and University Avenue corridors are known for restaurants, breweries and shops.

For appraisal purposes, North Park is one of the easier older neighborhoods to work in, because there are lots of sales. The challenge is matching quality. A bungalow restored with original woodwork and a new foundation is a different house from one that was flipped with laminate floors and an unpermitted addition. We look closely at condition, permits and lot use.

The housing stock

North Park's homes are mostly Craftsman and California bungalows, with Spanish Revival houses and some Prairie and Mission style homes, built roughly 1910 to 1940. Many sit on narrow lots, often 25 to 50 feet wide, with detached garages off alleys. The North Park Dryden Historic District has Craftsman homes built by David Owen Dryden. Postwar construction added stucco apartment buildings, including the boxy two-story Huffman six-packs of the 1960s and 1970s. Newer condos and mixed-use buildings line University Avenue and 30th Street. Many lots now have ADUs or granny flats.

Value drivers to watch around North Park

01

Restoration versus flip

Buyers here often pay more for original details like built-ins, wood windows and fireplaces. A quick remodel that strips those out may not add as much as its cost. We compare to homes with similar quality of work, not just similar square footage.

02

ADUs and alley lots

Alley access makes it easier to add a garage unit or ADU. A permitted, rented ADU can add real value. Unpermitted units may add little and can carry risk. We confirm permit status before giving any added space full credit.

03

Proximity to Balboa Park

Homes near Morley Field and the park's eastern edge often trade higher for the open space and quiet streets. Being near University Avenue and 30th Street brings walkability but also noise and parking pressure.

04

Historic districts

Homes inside designated historic districts, such as the Dryden district, face design review for exterior changes but may qualify for Mills Act property tax savings that pass to the next owner. We note any designation and compare to similar designated homes.

Appraisals people order here

North Park has many owners who bought decades back, which keeps probate, trust and inheritance valuations high on the list. Divorce appraisals are frequent for both bungalows and condos. Family-transfer appraisals help with Proposition 19 planning. Small multi-unit owners use 2-4 unit reports for buyouts. Pre-listing appraisals help sellers price a restored bungalow. With plenty of sales, a Desktop report often works for planning. When condition, permits or an ADU are in question, a Standard report with an inspection is the safer choice.

Asked about North Park

Does a permitted ADU raise my home's appraised value?

Usually, yes. We look at sales of similar homes with and without ADUs and at rental income when it is relevant. Unpermitted units are noted but usually get less weight, since a buyer may need to legalize or remove them.

Are North Park condos appraised differently from houses?

Yes. Condos are compared to other condos, ideally in the same building or similar buildings nearby. HOA dues, parking and building age matter more than lot features.

How do you treat a Huffman six-pack building?

These small apartment buildings are usually valued with the Standard 2-4 Unit approach if they have four or fewer units, or with income in mind. We compare to similar buildings and look at rents.
